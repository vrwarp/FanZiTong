# 繁字通 FanZiTong — critique from the heritage learner (iter1)

Who's talking: born in Taiwan, left after 2nd grade, speak like a 3rd grader, read pinyin fine, never learned Zhuyin. I want to read the 小吃店 slip, my parents' church bulletin, my cousins' LINE group and PTT without embarrassing myself, in 15–30 minutes a day on my phone. I reviewed all 20 screenshots (01–20). Findings are ordered by severity, one line each.

## Findings

- [blocker] [04-study-drill-cloze, 08-drill-menu-slip, 09-drill-menu-result, 10-drill-foil, 15-card-editor] The app grades 魯肉飯 as wrong, but half of Taiwan writes it that way — The cloze and the foil drill both offer 魯肉飯 as a trap answer, the order slip lists 滷肉飯 and 魯肉飯 as two separate dishes (and colors 魯肉飯 red on grading), and even the card editor's hint text proposes "魯 | 鹵" as foils for 滷. 鬍鬚張, the most famous 滷肉飯 chain in the country, spells its own name 鬍鬚張魯肉飯; the stall near my grandma's place writes 魯 too. The entire reason I opened this app is to read the real sign, so training me to flinch at 魯肉飯 teaches me the opposite of Taiwan, and it is exactly the moment I'd stop trusting the rest of the deck. Suggestion: add an "also written 常見寫法" field to the card (滷肉飯 → 魯肉飯), never generate foils from accepted variants, print the variant on the revealed card ("signs also write 魯肉飯"), and let the slip randomly use either spelling as the correct item. Keep the genuinely visual foils (滷內飯 is perfect; 鹵肉飯 is fine as a foil because you basically never see 鹵 on a sign).

- [major] [08-drill-menu-slip, 09-drill-menu-result] 蛋餅 is printed twice on the slip and the second one is graded as a mistake — Under 小菜 the slip lists 蛋餅 at row 2 and again at row 5. After I submit, the first turns green and the second turns red, alongside 魯肉飯 and 蚵仔炸, even though the verdict says "Perfect order". So if I had ticked the lower 蛋餅 I'd be marked wrong for ordering exactly what the order said, and on a perfect result I'm staring at four red rows wondering what I messed up. Suggestion: de-duplicate generated foils against the real items and against each other before rendering; on the result screen only color rows I ticked or missed, and show untouched look-alikes with a neutral "look-alike 形近" tag instead of red.

- [major] [14-vocab-list, 17-stats, 20-study-revealed-dark, 06-learn-after-session] The slang deck needs a Taiwan-usage audit — "Spoiler" is taught as 劇透. On PTT and in my cousins' group it's 雷: 有雷、爆雷、防雷、被雷到. 劇透 is the 中國用語 that people in Taiwan actively make fun of; if I used it my cousins would assume I learned Chinese from a mainland app. 藉口 sits in the Slang deck defined as "Excuse (Taiwan standard form)" — that's an ordinary everyday word, not slang, and the parenthetical reads like a dictionary footnote. And the domain is named 俚語, which to a Taiwanese ear means folk sayings and proverbs, not internet talk. Suggestion: rename the domain 網路用語 (or 流行語); make 爆雷／有雷 the card and mention 劇透 as "the China term you'll also see online"; move 藉口-type words into a 常用字 bucket; and have someone who lives on PTT/Dcard go through all 22 slang cards (母湯、是在哈囉、87、傻眼貓咪、北七、笑死、4ni are the real thing). Since PTT is full of ㄏㄏ／ㄎㄎ／頗ㄏ, I'd also take 3–4 cards that teach just those few Zhuyin letters used as slang — the one place I'd actually accept Zhuyin. The anime cards I can see (本命、中二、宅、抽卡、同人) are right; keep those.

- [major] [03-study-revealed, 13-drill-cloze-correct, 20-study-revealed-dark] The example sentence hands me its pinyin, so I never read it — After the reveal, the 例句 shows the characters, then a full pinyin line, then English. My eyes go straight to "Lǎobǎn, wǒ yào yī wǎn…" and skip the characters entirely, which is precisely what the app's own rule is trying to prevent. That sentence is the only connected reading in the whole flow, and I'm not reading it. Suggestion: show the sentence and the English by default, hide the sentence pinyin behind a small "拼音" tap (or reuse the reveal-delay setting). Same on the cloze result card.

- [major] [11-drill-foil-wrong] "不對 — the correct form is 滷肉飯" doesn't tell me what I got wrong — I picked 滷內飯. The feedback shows the right answer, but the whole point of this drill is that I can't see the difference between 內 and 肉 at reading speed, and the feedback leaves me squinting between two tiles. Suggestion: highlight the character that differs in both my pick and the answer (內 vs 肉) and add one line with the tell: "肉 has two 人 stacked inside; 內 has one 入". Do the same on the cloze wrong-answer state.

- [major] [19-learn-dark, 20-study-revealed-dark] Dark mode isn't dark — Both "dark" screenshots are the same cream-and-white as the light ones. I do this on the couch at 11pm; a full-screen white card with a 100px character is a flashlight. If picking Dark (or my phone's system dark) doesn't flip the theme, that's a bug I'd hit on night one. Suggestion: make sure the theme applies from both the in-app toggle and the phone's dark setting, and on the study card use a warm dark grey (not pure black) with off-white glyphs so the strokes stay crisp.

- [major] [08-drill-menu-slip] The order slip is the best idea in the app, but it's a greatest-hits menu, not a real shop — 阿婆小吃店, 桌號：3, the 小/大 columns, 嘴邊肉, 皮蛋豆腐, 蚵仔炸 as the trap for 蚵仔煎: all great. But no 滷肉飯 shop sells 蛋餅 (that's the 早餐店 next door), 蚵仔煎 and 肉圓 and 鹹酥雞 are three different night-market stalls, there's no 湯類 (貢丸湯、蛋花湯、味噌湯 are on every real slip), and there are no prices — reading 小30 大45 is half of what I actually do at the counter. The slip is also printed in the same sans font as the app, while every real slip is 標楷體 or 新細明體, and those are the shapes I need to recognise. And with a 20-second timer, the timer bar scrolls off the top and Submit is two screens down. Suggestion: build slips per shop type (滷肉飯／麵店: 飯類、麵類、湯類、小菜; 早餐店: 蛋餅、蘿蔔糕、豆漿、鐵板麵; 夜市 stall: one specialty plus drinks), add prices, render the slip in a bundled 楷體 or 明體 subset for the food characters, and pin the timer and Submit to the bottom of the screen.

- [minor] [04-study-drill-cloze, 10-drill-foil, 12-drill-cloze, 02-study-prompt-hidden] The answer tiles sit in the top half of the screen — On every drill the four tiles are in the upper-middle and the bottom third of the screen is empty. With one thumb on the couch I'm reaching up for A and B every time, while the recognition card correctly puts Again/Hard/Good/Easy at the bottom where my thumb already is. Suggestion: anchor the tile grid and Continue to the bottom of the viewport and let the sentence or cue sit above them; on the recognition card, make the whole screen the tap target so I don't have to aim at the top card.

- [minor] [05-session-summary, 06-learn-after-session, 17-stats, 18-settings, 07-drills-tab] The numbers don't add up and the words are Anki-speak — The summary says Time 2s and Retention 80% after five cards; the dashboard then shows Reviews 5/30 (I did zero reviews, those were new cards) next to a 100% Memory Health ring; Stats says 99% recall in one box and 80% retention in the next. Meanwhile I'm expected to understand "FSRS", "retrievability", "stability", "lapses", "leeches", "realia" and "Cloze Generator". I don't know what a leech is; it sounds like something wrong with my phone. Suggestion: count new cards under New only; hide Retention and Memory Health until there's a week of data ("not enough data yet"); fix the timer; and rename in plain language — "Keeps slipping 常忘的字" instead of Leeches, "Forgotten" for lapses, "Chance you remember it right now" for retrievability, "Fill in the blank" instead of Cloze Generator, and keep "FSRS" off the screens I see daily.

- [minor] [05-session-summary, 09-drill-menu-result, 13-drill-cloze-correct, 03-study-revealed] The Chinese copy sounds like a textbook, not like Taiwan — 做得好！ is how a dubbed cartoon praises you; people I know say 讚啦！、太強了！、辛苦了！ or just 今天練完了. 老闆說：沒問題！ — a real 老闆娘 says 好～馬上來！ or 好喔，等一下. 正確！ is what a computer says; a quiz host says 答對了！. Even the 例句 "我要一碗大碗的滷肉飯" is grammatical but stiff; at the counter it's "滷肉飯大碗一碗，加一顆滷蛋". Suggestion: give the app one consistent Taiwan voice (a little 台, a little lazy), rotate three or four phrases per moment, and keep the English small underneath. Small thing, but it's exactly the textbook-vs-Taiwan smell I notice.

- [minor] [01-learn-first-launch, 02-study-prompt-hidden, 03-study-revealed] First open is clear, with a few snags — The big red Start Daily Session and "≈ 8 min" tell me exactly what to do, good. But the "ready to work offline" toast lands right on top of YOUR DECK so I only see two of my four domains; the badge greets me with "🔥 Day 0", which is a strange hello; the hint says "tap the card" when there are two cards on screen; and on my very first reveal I'm looking at Again/Hard/Good/Easy with no idea what Hard versus Good means. Suggestion: move the offline notice to a bottom snackbar (or Settings); show "Start your streak" instead of Day 0; make the whole screen the tap target; and on the first three reveals add one line under the buttons: "Again = didn't get it · Hard = got it slowly · Good = got it · Easy = instant".

- [nit] [04-study-drill-cloze, 12-drill-cloze, 13-drill-cloze-correct, 07-drills-tab, 14-vocab-list] Small text things — The blank is always "_ _" even when the answer is three characters (滷肉飯); the filled sentence renders "牧師 和師母" with a space no Chinese sentence has; the header says "Which word completes the sentence?" while the box below says "Pick the character that fits"; the Drills domain dropdown truncates to "All active don"; and the vocab list cuts definitions at about 15 characters ("One's favorite ch…") so I can't tell what 本命 means without opening it. Suggestion: size the blank to the answer length, drop the spaces around the blank and the answer, pick one phrasing, widen the dropdown ("All domains"), and let list rows wrap to two lines.

## Overall

Overall score: 6/10 — the mechanics (tap-to-reveal, a real-feeling order slip, the actual church words) already feel built for someone like me, but the deck says things about Taiwan that aren't true (魯肉飯, 劇透), a grading bug and a non-working dark mode would hit me in the first two nights, and the stats speak a language I don't.

## Keep doing

- Pinyin stays hidden until I tap, and "Show pinyin" in the vocab list defaults to off. Never soften this.
- Characters are big and clean on the study card, and the forms are the Taiwan shapes (說、沒、遲 look right, not the Japanese variants).
- The order slip: 阿婆小吃店, 桌號：3, 小/大 columns, 嘴邊肉, 皮蛋豆腐, 蚵仔炸 versus 蚵仔煎. First app that made me feel like I'm standing at the counter. Build more of the app around this.
- Church words are the real words from my parents' church: 牧師、師母、牧者、關心年輕人.
- Example sentences that sound like people: 加一顆滷蛋 (顆!), 周邊全都收了, 一堆藉口.
- The visual foils 內/肉, 枚/收/牧, 師/帥 are exactly the blurs I actually have.
- "Use your ear for grammar" and "break the character-blur habit" talk to me as someone who already speaks, not a beginner.
- Rating buttons at the bottom with the next interval printed on them, sessions around 8 minutes, and Again cards coming back in the same session.
- Everything stays on my phone with a one-tap backup, and the footer tells me how to add it to the home screen on iPhone.

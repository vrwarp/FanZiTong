# 繁字通 FanZiTong — PM / user-journey critique (iter1)

Lens: senior PM, mobile learning apps. Judged from the 20 screenshots only — journeys, not code.

Pedagogy-rule check first: pinyin is hidden on every character prompt (02, 04, 08, 12; the vocab list ships with "Show pinyin" off) and appears only after the tap or the answer (03, 13, 20); the foil drill cues with sound + meaning by design (10). No violation found, so no blockers. Everything below is about whether the learner knows what to do, does it, and comes back tomorrow.

## Findings (ordered by severity)

- [major] [05-session-summary, 06-learn-after-session] The loop doesn't close: "Session complete" fires with half the day left, and nothing says what's next — The summary celebrates "🎉 Session complete / 做得好！" after 5 cards, but the dashboard the learner lands on says "DUE TODAY · 0 reviews, 5 new cards · ≈ 4 min" behind the identical "Start Daily Session" button they already pressed. There is no distinction between "daily goal reached" and "stopped early", no "Continue" path, and on neither screen an answer to "when do I come back?" — "🔥 Streak: Day 1" never says what keeps it alive, and the walkthrough has no all-done dashboard state at all. For a 15–30-min-a-day habit, the end-of-session moment is where you set tomorrow's appointment; right now it is a dead end. Suggest: (1) two summary variants — "Daily goal reached" vs "Session ended · 5 new cards still available today" with primary "Continue (≈ 4 min)" and secondary "Done for today"; (2) one line on the summary and in the DUE TODAY card once the queue is empty: "Next: 5 reviews due tomorrow — do them to keep the streak"; (3) design and ship the all-done dashboard state (CTA becomes "Done for today ✓ · Tomorrow: 5 reviews", with a secondary "Extra practice" link into Drills).

- [major] [03-study-revealed, 20-study-revealed-dark] The four rating buttons are the core input, and the app never says how to use them — After the reveal the learner faces "Again <10m · Hard <10m · Good 10m · Easy 8d" with no question stated ("Did you know the sound and meaning?") and no criteria. Two buttons carry the identical interval "<10m", so a first-timer literally cannot see what "Hard" does differently from "Again"; "Good 10m" vs "Easy 8d" is a jump of three orders of magnitude with no hint that Easy means "I read it instantly — skip the learning steps". Mis-grading in week one poisons the FSRS schedule, which the learner later experiences as "it keeps showing me words I know and hides the ones I don't". Suggest: a dismissable one-time coach strip above the buttons on the first three revealed cards ("Again = didn't get it · Hard = got it slowly or half · Good = got it · Easy = read it instantly"); a permanent one-line prompt "How well did you know it?" above the row; when two buttons resolve to the same interval, replace the duplicate with words ("Again · relearn now", "Hard · again soon"); a "New" badge on the card front (02) so a blank first attempt is expected and "Again" carries no shame.

- [major] [01-learn-first-launch] The first ten seconds talk about the app, not the learner — The very first message the product shows is a service-worker toast, "繁字通 is ready to work offline. You can add it to your home screen." with a "Close" button, sitting on top of YOUR DECK so two of the four domains are hidden. The streak chip reads "🔥 Day 0" (a flame next to a zero reads as failure, not invitation), the TODAY card shows "Reviews 0/30" when zero reviews can possibly exist, and MEMORY HEALTH shows "—" with no explanation. Nothing frames the method, so the learner reaches 02 and wonders why the app is hiding the pinyin they already know — the only explanation ("Pinyin is never shown with the prompt…") is buried in Settings. Suggest: replace the toast with a one-time three-line "How this works" card in the dashboard (1. You'll see a word in 繁體. 2. Say it in your head. 3. Tap to check — pinyin stays hidden until then, on purpose.) with a "Got it" that reveals the deck; move the install hint to a small non-blocking row under YOUR DECK, worded for benefit ("Add to Home Screen — opens full-screen and keeps your progress safe"); render the chip as "🔥 Start your streak today" until Day 1; hide the Reviews bar when none are due; show "Study to start tracking" in the empty gauge.

- [major] [05-session-summary, 06-learn-after-session, 17-stats, 19-learn-dark] The numbers don't reconcile, so the learner stops trusting them — Within two minutes the learner sees "RETENTION 80%" (summary), "100% RECALL / MEMORY HEALTH" (dashboard), then "99% RECALL / Average retrievability" and "30-day retention · 80% of answers were not 'Again'" (Stats). The dashboard gauge and the Stats gauge are the same metric under different names. The summary tiles "CARDS REVIEWED 5" and "ANSWERS 5" are twins with no hint of why they would ever differ. After studying only new cards the dashboard says "Reviews 5/30" (red bar) beside "DUE TODAY · 0 reviews" — "reviews" means answers-given in one card and cards-due in the other, and the red bar reads as a warning while "New 5/10" is green. Stats' domain section ("Cards with stability over 30 days") shows "0/22 · 0%" for all four domains and will stay at 0% for weeks, the opposite of motivating on day one. Suggest: one headline metric with one name everywhere ("Recall now" — the retrievability number) and one secondary ("Not-Again rate, 30 days"); relabel the summary tiles "Cards 5 · Answers 5 (incl. repeats)" and "First-try correct 4/5" instead of "RETENTION"; rename the dashboard bar "Answers today 5/30" or count only due reviews; one neutral colour for both bars; show per-domain progress as started / learning / solid so day-1 effort is visible.

- [major] [08-drill-menu-slip, 09-drill-menu-result] The order-slip drill can be solved by matching shapes, and the slip contains an identical duplicate row — The cue "Order 點餐： 滷肉飯 (小)、蛋餅、蚵仔煎" is written in the very characters the learner must find on the slip, so the task collapses into "spot the same glyphs" rather than reading — in the one drill whose realia framing should make reading unavoidable. In 小菜, "蛋餅" appears twice; the result screen ticks the first as correct and tints the second red as a trap. An identical string as a trap under a 20-second timer is unfair, and the learner cannot know which one counts. The red rows in the result (魯肉飯, second 蛋餅, 蚵仔炸) also appear on a "Perfect order" with no legend, which reads as "I got something wrong". Suggest: give the order the way a customer says it — as meaning ("braised pork rice, small · egg crepe · oyster omelette") — so the learner must read the slip (the foil drill already establishes the pattern of cueing with something other than the glyphs); de-duplicate foils against the target strings and against each other before rendering; in the result, tint only rows the learner wrongly ticked, or label tinted rows "look-alike trap you avoided".

- [major] [08-drill-menu-slip] Under a 20-second timer, the order and the clock scroll away — The slip runs 20 rows across four sections (the capture is a 3354-px full page); the order line and the "20s" bar sit at the top, so the moment the learner scrolls to 小菜 / 小吃 they can neither re-read what was ordered nor see time remaining, and "Submit order 送單" is reachable only at the bottom. Time-boxed tasks must keep the brief and the clock in view. Suggest: a compact order + timer strip pinned to the top; "N boxes ticked · Submit order" pinned to the bottom; consider trimming the slip to ~12 rows in three sections — still authentic, and readable inside 20 s.

- [major] [11-drill-foil-wrong, 13-drill-cloze-correct, 09-drill-menu-result] Wrong-answer feedback doesn't teach, and feedback depth differs per drill — After picking 滷內飯 the learner gets "不對 — the correct form is 滷肉飯". For someone whose whole problem is character blur, the app never points out that the difference is 內 vs 肉. Meanwhile the cloze's correct-answer card is generous ("正確！ Correct", "mù shī · Pastor", sentence pinyin, translation) and the menu drill's is a checklist — three drills, three feedback shapes, and the moment that matters most (being wrong in the foil drill) gets the thinnest one. Suggest: a shared feedback card for all drills (verdict → the word, large → why → example); for a foil miss, render the chosen and correct words side by side at prompt size with the differing character highlighted and one line ("second character is 肉 'meat', not 內 'inside'" — the editor's foil field already knows which component differs); add "This word will come back sooner" so the learner sees the consequence.

- [major] [19-learn-dark, 20-study-revealed-dark, 18-settings] Selecting "Dark" appears to do nothing (conditional on the capture being correct) — Both screens labelled dark render with the same cream background, white cards and black text as 06 and 03, and the Settings capture shows "System" selected. If the captures were taken with the Dark button pressed, the toggle is broken from the user's point of view — a control that visibly does nothing is worse than no control, and study-in-bed is a top use case for a 15-minute habit app. If they were taken in System mode, the walkthrough is mislabelled. Either way: verify on a real device, and make the Theme buttons show a selected state that changes on tap.

- [minor] [07-drills-tab, 08, 10, 12, 02] Every drill has three names, and the chrome speaks SRS jargon — The tab card "Menu Simulation" becomes kicker "ORDER SLIP · 點菜單" and header "Menu realia"; "Cloze Generator" becomes "FILL IN THE BLANK" and "Cloze"; "Foil Drill" becomes "SPOT THE RIGHT CHARACTER" and "Foil discrimination". "Cloze Generator" and "realia" are teacher words. The tab subtitle "Standalone practice. Results still feed your FSRS schedule." assumes the learner knows what FSRS is; so do "Memory health, lapses and leeches" (Stats), "Leech at lapses" (Settings) and "Relearning". The domain select truncates to "All active don". Exits are doubled ("← Back" plus "End session" / "End") without saying how they differ; progress is written three ways ("0 answered · 9 left", "Card 1/10", "2 left after this"); the cloze labels options A–D, the foil drill doesn't; session cards carry a domain chip ("🍜 Food / 飲食"), drills don't. Suggest: one plain name per drill used in all three places (Order Slip 點菜單 · Fill the Blank 填空 · Spot the Character 辨字); subtitle "Extra practice — answers here also change when each word comes back for review"; plain-language stats labels with the term once in parentheses ("Words you keep forgetting (leeches)"); one exit control per screen; one progress format ("3 of 10"); "All domains" in the select.

- [minor] [04-study-drill-cloze, 12-drill-cloze, 10-drill-foil] The cloze is the foil drill wearing a sentence — The mid-session cloze offers exactly the option set the foil drill uses (鹵肉飯 / 魯肉飯 / 滷內飯 / 滷肉飯), and the standalone cloze does the same (枚師 / 收師 / 牧師 / 牧帥). The Drills tab promises "Use your ear for grammar to pick the correctly written word", but no ear separates homophone look-alikes — the sentence is irrelevant to the answer, so two of the three modes are one task in different chrome, which the learner will notice within a week. The hint card under the options says "Pick the character that fits." (the answer is a two- or three-character word) inside a card several times taller than its text, above a disabled pink "Continue". Suggest: give the cloze at least one real, readable distractor from the deck that fits the grammar but not the sentence (e.g. 蛋餅 or 珍珠奶茶 in the 一碗大碗的 __ slot — the measure word rules them out, which is exactly the "ear for grammar" the copy promises), keeping look-alike foils for the foil drill; fix the copy to "Pick the word that fits"; collapse the hint to one line and hide "Continue" until an answer is chosen.

- [minor] [15-card-editor, 18-settings] Destructive actions look like primary actions, and the backup story has no reminder — In the editor "Delete" sits beside "Save" as an equal-weight filled red button (fat-finger distance on a phone); in Settings "Reset all data" is a filled red button in the same style as "Start Daily Session", directly under "Restore backup". Settings says "Everything lives in this browser. Back up regularly", but nothing shows when the last backup happened, and a learner who loses months of FSRS state to a cleared browser does not come back. Suggest: make Delete a text link at the bottom of the editor and Reset an outlined danger button, both behind a confirm that names the loss ("Delete 本命 and its review history?"); show "Last backup: never / 12 days ago" beside the buttons and a gentle dashboard nudge after 14 study days without one; hide "Stability 0.00 days / Difficulty 0.00 / 10" for cards in state New ("Not studied yet").

- [minor] [14-vocab-list] Data plumbing outranks the learner's jobs on the Vocab tab — Above the search field sit four data actions ("Import CSV / JSON", "Export JSON", "Export CSV", "Load starter deck"); the last is unexplained on a deck that already contains the starter deck (duplicate? reset? add missing?). Definitions truncate early ("One's favorite ch…", "Chūnibyō (edgy t…") because the domain pill takes a third of the row, and the list opens on Anime with no visible sort. Suggest: search and filters first; collapse import/export into a "⋯" menu or point to Settings › Data, where backup already lives; rename to "Restore starter deck (adds N missing cards)" with a live count; replace the pill with a small coloured dot so the definition fits; add a sort control (Due · Newest · Domain).

Overall score: 6/10 — The skeleton of a good daily loop is here (one CTA, an honest time estimate, the pedagogy rule respected on every prompt), but the loop doesn't close, the rating buttons and drill feedback leave the learner guessing, and three different "health" numbers within minutes leak trust and habit.

## Keep doing

- Pinyin hidden on every character prompt by default, with "Show pinyin" off in the vocab list and the Settings copy explaining why — the rule is applied consistently and honestly.
- The dashboard leads with one clear action and a time estimate ("0 reviews, 10 new cards · ≈ 8 min").
- Rating buttons preview their interval — "Easy 8d" tells the learner what the button costs.
- Bilingual chrome (Learn 學習, Drills 練習, FILL IN THE BLANK · 填空, Submit order 送單) puts characters in front of the learner even outside the cards.
- The order slip is delightful and authentic — 阿婆小吃店, 桌號：3, 小/大 columns, "老闆說：沒問題！".
- Greying out unchosen options with green/red states on foil and cloze results reads at a glance.
- Import preview with New / Duplicates / Repeated-in-file counts and a CTA that states its effect ("Import 2 cards").
- Editor helper text is learner-oriented ("Must contain the word to power cloze drills", foil examples 魯 | 鱸).
- Starter deck loaded on first open — no empty-deck dead end on day one.
- Plain data copy ("Everything lives in this browser") — keep the honesty; add the reminder.

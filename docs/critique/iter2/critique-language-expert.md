## (b) New findings (ordered by severity)

- [major] [04-study-drill-cloze / 12-drill-cloze / 07-drills-tab] **Fill the Blank now has false keys** — In both captured items a second tile fits the sentence perfectly: 「老闆，餛飩湯大碗一碗，加一顆滷蛋。」 is an ordinary order, and 「我們牧師每週三在弟兄家聚會。」 is an ordinary sentence. The hint card "Only one option fits the grammar and the meaning" and the tab copy "your ear for grammar rules out the rest" are therefore false, and this learner's ear is better than the generator. Why it matters: an item with two defensible keys punishes exactly the behaviour the app exists to build — reading the options accurately and trusting the reading — and under B5 ("wrong → Again for any state") a false-key miss drags a stable Review card back into relearning. This drill reaches the daily session every fifth card, so it is not a side feature. Under the old design (four misspellings) this could not happen; B4 introduced real words without a uniqueness guarantee. Fix, all offline: (a) make meaning the cue on demand — a `Show meaning 意思` pill on the prompt reveals the sentence's English translation (the sanctioned meaning-as-cue exception; it makes the key unique by construction); using it caps the outcome at "no schedule change" (Learning cards: Hard), a desirable difficulty with an honest escape hatch; (b) if you prefer an unscaffolded prompt, draw the two deck words from other domains — 牧師 in an order sentence is excluded by meaning but still has to be read to be rejected, which is the bottleneck skill; same-domain distractors are only safe when the section tag differs and the sentence is not an order/collocation template (the food deck now has section tags; use them); (c) grade by the evidence about the target: choosing a real deck word says nothing about whether the target's shape is bound, so reply "That reads 牧師 mù shī 'pastor' — the sentence was about the 小組", apply no FSRS change and re-ask; only the foil pick (小阻) is a miss on the target; (d) never build a cloze on a sentence shown at a reveal earlier in the same session — run Spot the Character instead (04 is 03's sentence five cards later); (e) delete the uniqueness claim from the hint card and the tab.

- [major] [03-study-revealed / 20-study-revealed-dark / 02-study-prompt-hidden] **The rating asks about knowing, and the new-card copy tells the learner not to try** — "How well did you know it?" (03, 20) invites a judgment about the word, which this learner knows by sound and meaning in almost every case; the construct being scheduled is the reading. After the three coach-strip reveals the anchors vanish and the bare question stands alone next to the schedule buttons (20). On the prompt face a new card says "New word: a blank is expected, just tap." (02) — an explicit licence to skip the retrieval attempt, when the attempt itself, even when it fails, is what makes the subsequent reveal stick (pretesting effect: Kornell, Hays & Bjork 2009; Richland, Kornell & Kao 2009), and a heritage reader can often read a "new" card outright because "new" only means new to the app. Principle: judgments of learning are cue-driven and the wording chooses the cue (Koriat 1997); "know" cues the aural lexicon → systematic over-rating → intervals too long → lapses → leeches. Fix: question → `How well did you read it? 讀得如何？`; new-card line → `New here — try to read it before you tap 先試著唸出來`; keep `?` and the anchors, and consider one persistent grey anchor under the row, the one this learner needs most: `Hard = slow, or only part of it`.

- [minor] [11-drill-foil-wrong] **The corrective tap is a copy-match, not a re-retrieval** — After the contrast card the correct tile is already outlined green, the other tiles are greyed, and the instruction prints the answer: "Now tap 滷肉飯 to continue." The learner taps the green box; nothing is retrieved. An error is corrected far better when feedback is followed by a successful retrieval attempt than by a matching action (Kornell, Klein & Rawson 2015; Metcalfe 2017). Fix: keep the contrast card (the answer must be shown), but reset the tile row — reshuffle, no outlines, nothing greyed — and ask `Now find lǔ ròu fàn again 再找一次` (sound cue only); outline green only after a correct tap and re-show the contrast on a second miss; keep the planned end-of-drill re-queue and announce it ("You'll see this one again before the end").

- [minor] [09-drill-menu-result] **Slip miss feedback stays at word level and the wrong tick is unglossed** — "❌ 蚵仔煎 kē zǎi jiān — Marked Again" says nothing about 蚵仔炸 two rows above it, although that look-alike is the most likely cause of the miss, and "Ticked by mistake: 臭豆腐" gives no reading, although a wrong tick is precisely a shape the learner mis-bound. The charInfo tells exist (11 proves it); the slip does not use them, so the most transfer-relevant surface gets the least elaborative feedback. Fix: when a missed target has a look-alike row on the slip, add the FeedbackCard "why" line under it — `煎 jiān (pan-fried, 灬 underneath) — not 炸 zhá (deep-fried, 火 beside 乍)`; print reading and gloss inline for every wrongly ticked row (`臭豆腐 chòu dòu fǔ · stinky tofu — not ordered`) — push feedback on errors, leave tap-to-reveal for correct and untouched rows. Also keep the size columns out of the schedule: a right dish in the wrong 小/大 column is a slip error for the verdict but not an Again on the word, because 小/大 is not the construct being scheduled.

- [minor] [13-drill-cloze-correct] **Correct-pick feedback leaves the look-alike unexplained and gives the distractor readings away** — After a correct pick 小阻 is simply greyed: the learner never learns that 阻 is zǔ "block, hinder" (阝) while 組 is zǔ "group" (糹). A homophone foil is the single best teaching moment in the deck because sound cannot separate the pair — only the radical can — and the learner already owns zǔzhǐ aurally, so the binding is free. Meanwhile the two real distractors are printed with pinyin ("牧師 mù shī · Pastor"), which is exposure, not the "free retrieval per tile" the plan intended. Feedback after correct answers still raises retention, especially for low-confidence correct answers (Butler, Karpicke & Roediger 2008). Fix: label the foil tile `形近 look-alike` with the one-line tell from charInfo (`阻 zǔ "block" — 組 has 糹`); show the deck-word tiles' readings on tap (`牧師 · tap to check`) with a `Show all` second step; log the taps.

- [minor] [03-study-revealed / 13-drill-cloze-correct / 20-study-revealed-dark] **"Show reading" reveals the whole sentence at once** — Compliant with the rule, but one tap turns the only connected-text retrieval per card back into the round-1 situation, opt-in: the eye drops to the romanised line and the characters are skipped. The stored sentence pinyin is already word-segmented ("Lǎobǎn, lǔròufàn dà wǎn yī wǎn, jiā yī kē lǔdàn", 15), so per-word alignment is a small offline job: split each token into syllables (one tone-bearing nucleus each) and walk the characters, skipping punctuation. Fix: tap a word in the sentence → its reading appears under that word only; the pill becomes `Show all readings` as a second step; record which words get tapped — those are the characters the learner cannot yet read in context, the cheapest leech-prevention signal available without new data.

- [minor] [17-stats / 20-study-revealed-dark] **Leech remediation is deferred, but the cheap half is already possible** — The leech row still offers only "Practice Difficult Characters" (which launches ordinary word-level drills), and the leech's review card (20) is indistinguishable from any other card. The full remediation view rightly waits for `chosen` logs, but charInfo and variants have shipped: the row can already show the per-character chips (藉 jiè · 口 kǒu), the variant line (也寫作 借口 — so a typed 借口 is not a miss), and the card's foils with their tells; and the review card of any card at or above the "Flag after N forgets" threshold (18) can open the per-character popovers pre-expanded after the reveal. Rename the button `Practice this word` and launch Spot the Character on that card and its foils only. Principle: a leech is a diagnosis of interference, which needs a discriminating cue, not more undifferentiated exposures.

- [minor] [20-study-revealed-dark / 03-study-revealed / 17-stats] **The variant note is boilerplate and the definition carries orthographic metadata** — "Also written 借口 — common on signs and menus." (20) is false for 借口, which is common in typed chat and in mainland text and never on a menu; the same string is true for 魯肉飯 (03). The definition "Excuse (Taiwan standard form)" puts spelling information where the meaning belongs and gets truncated to "Excuse (Taiwan standard …" on the leech row (17). Where a variant occurs — sign, menu, chat, subtitle — is part of what the learner needs to know about it; medium awareness is part of orthographic knowledge. Fix: `variantNote?: string` per card with a domain default (food: "common on signs and menus"; slang/church/anime: "common in chats and online"); definition → `Excuse`; the "Taiwan standard" fact moves to the variant line: `藉口 is the Taiwan standard spelling; 借口 is what most people type`.

- [minor] [15-card-editor] **The foil helper still teaches the mistake B3 banned** — "Look-alike characters/words for discrimination drills, separated with | (e.g. 鹵 | 鱸)." 鹵 is the attested spelling the plan removed from the foil pool, and this helper is the only guidance a learner gets when authoring a card from a menu or a bulletin, so the negative-transfer problem will be re-created card by card. Fix: `Wrong look-alikes only, separated with | (e.g. 滷內飯 | 滷肉販). Real alternative spellings go under Also written.`; at save time reject a foil that equals any card's headword or variant with "That is a real spelling — move it to Also written".

- [nit] [03-study-revealed] **Character facts on the reveal are not connected** — The reveal shows three separate character-level facts (the 滷 lǔ +1 chip; 滷蛋 in the sentence; 也寫作 魯肉飯) that never point at each other, and "+1" is unexplained. Orthographic representations consolidate when the same character is noticed across words (Share's self-teaching account applied at the character grain), and the sentence already contains the evidence. Fix: tapping a chip underlines every occurrence of that character in the sentence in the chip's colour and lists the "also in" words as characters first (滷蛋、滷味) with tap-to-reveal readings; label the count `also in 1 word`.

## (c) Score and convergence

Overall score: 8/10 — the daily loop (character-only prompt with no domain cue, tap-only sentence reading, character chips, anchored rating), Spot the Character (character-level contrast with a gated corrective step) and the Order Slip (sound + meaning cue, honest grading, asymmetric scheduling) now place real retrieval demand on the bottleneck and deliver feedback at the character grain; what holds the score down is Fill the Blank's false keys, which reach the daily session every fifth card and can wrongly reset a Review card, and a rating question that asks about knowing rather than reading.

Converged? Yes for shape → known-word binding in the daily loop, Spot the Character and the Order Slip; the biggest remaining gap is Fill the Blank, whose deck-word distractors can legitimately fit the sentence, so a learner who reads correctly is marked wrong and rescheduled.

## Keep doing
- The "How this works" card (01): "Say it in your head: the sound and the meaning you already know" is the right framing of the method for a heritage speaker, and it explains the pinyin rule in one sentence.
- The Spot the Character contrast card (11): side-by-side words, the differing character outlined, reading + gloss for both, and a tell — this is the template for every feedback moment in the app.
- Sound + meaning as the slip cue with the Taiwanese name (ô-á-chian) alongside the Mandarin reading (08); look-alike traps adjacent to their targets (鹽肉飯 above 滷肉飯, 半肉麵 above 牛肉麵).
- Honest slip grading (09): green / amber / red / grey tags that mean four different things, a per-target schedule line, and tap-to-reveal for every row.
- The rating anchors ("Hard = got it slowly or only part of it", "Easy = instant, like reading a chat") and the visible reveal latency (03).
- "One more look — tap to check" at session end, in characters only (05).
- Variants as first-class data: 也寫作 in the reveal, the list and the editor, never as a wrong answer (03, 14, 15).
- Per-character chips on the reveal (03, 20) — extend them into the sentence and into leech remediation.
- Generic strategy hints before the answer ("Look at each character, not the silhouette", 10) with specific component notes only after it.

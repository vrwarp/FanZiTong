## B. New findings (ordered by severity)

- [major] [04-study-drill-cloze, 12-drill-cloze] Fill the Blank now marks a correct reading wrong — the "readable" distractors fit the sentence too — Both captured items have a second valid answer. 04: "老闆，＿＿＿大碗一碗，加一顆滷蛋。" offers 餛飩湯 beside 滷肉飯; a large wonton soup with a braised egg is a completely normal order. 12: "我們＿＿每週三在弟兄家聚會。" offers 牧師 beside 小組; "our pastor meets at a brother's home every Wednesday" is a perfectly good sentence. The hint card says "Only one option fits the grammar and the meaning." and the Drills tab promises "your ear for grammar rules out the rest" — both are false here. This matters more in this product than in a generic app: the learner's whole premise is "you already know the language, we only teach the shapes", so being told they are wrong about grammar/meaning by the app is the one thing guaranteed to break trust, and it will happen within the first week (the cloze fires every 5th card). Suggest: (1) author per-card "wrong-but-readable" options (2 words) for the starter deck, with an editor field under Example sentence ("Cloze distractors 填空干擾詞 — must NOT fit this sentence"); (2) generator fallback when none are authored: draw the two readable distractors from a different domain (蛋餅 or 讀經 in a 一碗 slot is unambiguously wrong yet still has to be read), keeping the one same-word look-alike; (3) change the hint to "Which word goes here?" until every item is guaranteed single-answer; (4) after a wrong pick, show the English translation as a second cue so the learner can recover by reading rather than guessing.

- [major] [05-session-summary, 06-learn-after-session, 19-learn-dark] "End session" → "Session paused" → "Done for today" → the dashboard forgets all of it — The header control says "End session"; the screen it opens is titled "Session paused"; the secondary button says "Done for today"; and the Learn tab afterwards (06) shows the pre-session card again: "DUE TODAY · 0 reviews, 5 new cards · Start Daily Session" — no "Resume · 7 cards", no "✓ studied today", no tomorrow line, and the streak chip "🔥 Day 1" never says whether today is secured. The learner who chose "Done for today" is immediately nagged to start again; the learner who wanted to resume finds no way back to the 7 waiting cards. Two rounds in, the all-done dashboard state has still never been captured. Suggest: rename the header control "Pause"; persist the paused queue and make the dashboard CTA "Resume session · 7 cards · ≈ 5 min" (with a small "Start over"); make "Done for today" actually flip the dashboard into a done state — outlined "Done for today ✓ · 5 words studied", sub-line "Tomorrow: 1 review + up to 10 new · ≈ 8 min", secondary "Study 5 more" and "Extra practice →", chip "🔥 Day 1 ✓"; and add a walkthrough step that finishes a session so the empty-queue state can be reviewed.

- [minor] [03-study-revealed, 20-study-revealed-dark] The rubric sits under the buttons at the last 40 px of the screen, and the whole rating row is in-flow — On 03 the rubric ("Again = didn't get it · Hard = …") is rendered below the four buttons, touching the bottom edge of the viewport, on the very card that introduces it; a first-timer will rate before reading. The buttons themselves are the last thing in the scroll (03 puts them at ~88 % of the screen height with a two-line definition and a one-line sentence); a card with a three-line church definition or a longer 例句 pushes the most-used control in the app below the fold, so every such card costs a scroll. "took 0.4s" appears with no label and, on 03, no "?" to explain it. Suggest: make "How well did you know it?" + the four buttons a sticky footer (safe-area padded) with the answer panel scrolling beneath; put the rubric above the buttons for the first three reveals, or as sublabels inside them ("Again · didn't get it"); label the latency "0.4 s to answer" or show it only from the fourth card.

- [minor] [05-session-summary, 06-learn-after-session, 19-learn-dark, 04-study-drill-cloze] "Due" and "left" mean different things on adjacent screens — 05 says "Next: 1 review due within a day — do them to keep the streak"; 06, seconds later, says "0 reviews … No reviews due yet."; 19, later, says "1 review". The mid-session header on 04 says "5 answered · 6 left" while the summary that follows says "7 cards still waiting". "do them" for one review. None is wrong on its own, but the learner sees 1 → 0 → 1 and 6 → 7 and concludes the counters are approximate — which undoes the trust work done this round. Suggest: one definition of due on the dashboard = due by end of today ("1 review · 5 new"), with "later today" wording if a card isn't due yet; count "left" identically in the session header and the summary (remaining cards including the current one, drills excluded); pluralise ("do it / do them").

- [minor] [09-drill-menu-result] The verdict is below an 11-row slip, the rows that matter are colour-only, and the consequence line speaks Anki — On submit the learner is looking at the slip; the verdict card ("老闆娘：欸，你是不是點錯了？ Not quite.") and the per-item results are below the entire slip, so the moment of judgement is off-screen. Untouched look-alike rows get a tag ("形近 look-alike") but the amber missed row (蚵仔煎) and the red wrongly-ticked row (臭豆腐) — the two the learner needs — get colour only. "Tap any dish on the slip to see how it is read" is text under the slip; nothing on the rows says they are now tappable. "Marked Good — one step closer to graduating." and "Already in review — schedule unchanged." are SRS jargon. The header still reads "Order Slip · 1 to go" after grading. Suggest: on submit, turn the sticky cue strip into a verdict strip ("Not quite · 2 of 3 · 1 wrong tick") and scroll the first amber/red row into view; tag those rows "Missed 漏點" / "Wrong 點錯" and auto-expand their reading line; reword to "Good — moves it toward long-term review" and "Already learned — no change"; header "Slip 1 of 1 · done".

- [minor] [14-vocab-list, 17-stats, 09-drill-menu-result, 01-learn-first-launch] Card states and the recall metric still have two or three names each — States: Vocab rows say "New / Learning / Review" (14), the Stats legend says "Not started / Learning / Reviewing / Relearning" (17), the slip says "Already in review" (09). The gauge is "RECALL" on the dashboard (its caption is hidden under the tab bar on 01/06/19) and "Average retrievability" in Stats, and it shows "—" on both with no visible reason; the tiles say "LAPSES 4 · LEECHES 1"; "30-day retention 80%" is derived from 5 answers; the domain caption "Cards with stability over 30 days." now sits above bars that count _learning_ cards. Suggest: one state vocabulary everywhere (New · Learning · Review · Relearning), one metric name ("Recall now") with "Average retrievability" demoted to a caption, an explicit empty state on both gauges ("Shows after 7 study days · 1 so far") that is visible above the tab bar, "Not enough answers yet (5/10)" under ten answers, tiles "Forgotten 4 · Keeps slipping 1", caption "Solid = you'd still read it after a month".

- [minor] [20-study-revealed-dark, 03-study-revealed, 15-card-editor] Reveal-panel copy that is right for food and wrong for everything else — 藉口's panel reads "Also written 借口 — common on signs and menus." — the suffix is a food string applied to every card; an abstract word about excuses is not on any menu. The per-character chips "滷 lǔ +1 · 肉 ròu +2 · 飯 fàn +1" (03) carry an unexplained "+N" (a count of other deck words, presumably) that reads like a score, and the chips look like tags, not buttons. The editor labels the same field "Also written 常見寫法" (15) while the list and the reveal say "也寫作". Suggest: generic suffix "— you'll see both spellings", with an optional per-card note; chips as "肉 ròu · 2 more words" plus a one-line caption "Tap a character for its meaning and other words"; one Chinese label (也寫作) in editor, list and reveal.

- [minor] [01-learn-first-launch, 06-learn-after-session, 19-learn-dark] First impression: the app's own name wraps, and the explainer never leaves — On the very first screen the wide "🔥 Start your streak" chip forces the title onto two lines as "FanZiTong 繁 / 字通" — the product's name split mid-word is the first thing the learner sees (06 with "🔥 Day 1" fits; a long streak "🔥 Day 120" will wrap again). The "HOW THIS WORKS" card is still on the dashboard after a full session (06, 19); once the learner has done the loop the card is just 40 % of the screen between them and the CTA. Suggest: keep 繁字通 unbreakable and let the chip shrink ("🔥 Start" / icon-only under ~380 px); auto-dismiss the explainer after the first session ends or pauses, not only on "Got it".

- [minor] [14-vocab-list, 16-import-preview] Import, export and "restore starter deck" have no visible entry point on the Vocab tab — Moving the data actions out of the header was the right call, but 14 shows only "+ Add"; there is no "⋯" menu, and 16 (the import dialog) opens from somewhere the capture doesn't reveal. If the actions live under "+ Add" or at the foot of a 95-row list, a learner who wants to import a church word-list will not find them. Suggest: "+ Add ▾" with "Add a card / Import CSV or JSON / Restore starter deck", and a one-line footer under the list: "Backups: Settings › Data".

- [nit] [07-drills-tab, 08-drill-menu-slip, 17-stats, 10-drill-foil, 12-drill-cloze] Small inconsistencies — "Hear the word, pick the right shape" (07) implies audio; the cue is pinyin + meaning (say "Read the sound and meaning"). With Questions = 5 the slip header says "Order Slip · 1 to go" (08) — either the setting doesn't apply to the slip or the format hides it; use "Slip 1 of 1". The 30-day chart's last two x-labels overprint as "09-0509-06" (17). "內用 ☐ 外帶 ☐" on the slip look tickable under a 20-second timer — render them as printed text. ## C. Score and judgement

Overall score: 7/10 — The daily loop now reads as one design: a clear first screen, a prompt that says what to do, a reveal with a rubric and character-level teaching, drills that explain the miss, and a summary with a real next step; what holds it back is one drill that marks correct readings wrong and a dashboard that forgets what the learner just chose.

Converged? **Not yet — but one short round away.** I would not ship tomorrow: Fill the Blank will tell this particular learner they are wrong about a sentence they know is right (B1), and that is the app's premise inverted; fix the distractor rule and the post-session handoff (B2), and I would ship without waiting for the rest.

## Keep doing

- Pinyin behind a tap everywhere, now including the example sentence; the rule is applied consistently and the "How this works" card explains why.
- The foil-miss card (11) is the best screen in the app: side-by-side words, the differing character named and glossed, a memorable tell, and Continue gated on tapping the right one.
- Cue-by-sound on the order slip with a pinned brief and pinned Submit; honest colouring with "形近 look-alike" tags.
- Rating row: question, latency, interval on every button, rubric on the first cards and "?" afterwards; no more duplicate intervals.
- Paused summary with "Continue session" / "Done for today", "One more look" chips, and a next-appointment line — the right skeleton; it just needs the dashboard to honour it.
- One name per drill across tab, kicker and header; single exit control; "All domains".
- Vocab tab in the learner's order (search → sort → filters), definitions in full, "也寫作" sub-line, "Show pinyin" off.
- Settings: "Last backup: never", storage used, outlined "Reset all data", plain "Flag after N forgets".

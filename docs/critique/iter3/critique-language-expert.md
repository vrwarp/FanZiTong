## (c) New findings

- [major] [13-drill-foil-wrong / 17-drill-cloze-correct] **The character tell is missing for both captured foil pairs** — 13 says only "午 is not 牛" (no readings, no glosses, no component note) and 17 tags 見正 "形近 look-alike" with nothing after it. In round 2 the same card said "內 nèi 'inside' is not 肉 ròu 'meat' — 肉 has two 人 stacked inside; 內 has one 入"; that line is what made it the best screen in the app, and without it the feedback is verification-only again: the learner is told which position differs and nothing about why — the round-1 defect, back. These are not obscure pairs. 午/牛 is the canonical stroke-length confusable (牛字出頭，午字不出頭 — in 牛 the vertical breaks through the top bar, in 午 it stops under it), and 正/證 is a homophone pair (both zhèng), where sound cannot help and only the 言 can — exactly the case item 10 said must "say so in the tell". Fix: author charInfo for 午 (wǔ "noon"), 牛 (niú "cow") and 正 (zhèng "upright; correct") alongside 證 (zhèng "prove; testimony" — 言 + 登, same sound, so look for the 言); make item 10's test enumerate the differing character of every `visualFoils` entry and every cloze foil in the starter deck and fail the build on a missing entry; and for user-authored cards without an entry, fall back to the whole-word reading and gloss of both tiles rather than a bare "X is not Y".

- [major] [10-drill-menu-slip / 11-drill-menu-result] **便當 has no neighbour, and sits in the wrong section at the wrong price** — the 小菜 section is 便當 35 / 燙花枝 80 / 涼拌小黃瓜 35. The neighbour rule evidently does not fire for a target that `categorizeDish` cannot place. Fix: give 便當 its own block, the way real 便當店 slips do — 排骨便當 / 雞腿便當 / 焢肉便當 / 魚排便當 at 90–120 (排骨 is already a deck word, 18) — so every neighbour shares 便當 and the differing head (排骨 / 雞腿 / 焢肉) is what must be read; add a test that every target gets a same-section neighbour, including targets that fall through to 小菜; enforce the ≤ 14-row cap (10 has 16).
